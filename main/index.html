NITANGOJA

Below the starry skies a beauty dance,
Love struck, I fall with every glance,
With eyes and a smile full of grace,
An eternity I long to spend in her embrace,

Miranda, a beauty in whose eyes I wander,
With all in heaven as your admirers,
In love’s sweet tides, this king descends ,
To weave with you a story that never ends.

Crowned my queen by heaven’s design,
No jewel more precious, no star more radiant,
At your feet I lay this heart of mine,
Woelinam,would you forever be mine?

I yearn for your touch like the desert for rain,
My heart aches for you as the light of night draw near,
In dreams I wander the night’s lonely seas,
Longing for you to come back to me.

your soft skin
